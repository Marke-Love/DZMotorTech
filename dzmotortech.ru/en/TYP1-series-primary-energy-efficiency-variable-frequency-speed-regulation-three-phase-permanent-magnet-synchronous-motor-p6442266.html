TYP1 series primary energy efficiency variable frequency speed regulation three-phase permanent magnet synchronous motor


Application Scenarios

Widely used in various industrial equipment requiring variable frequency speed regulation, such as CNC machine tools, injection molding machines, textile machinery, printing machinery, packaging machinery, conveying machinery, etc., it is an ideal high-efficiency drive solution in industrial automation systems.

Product Certification and Guarantee

This series of motors has passed ISO9001 Quality Management System certification, China Energy Conservation Product Certification and CE certification, and complies with GB/T 755 Rotating Electrical Machines – Rating and Performance and IEC 60034 international standards. The company has an annual production capacity of 500,000 motors, which can ensure fast delivery, and provides an 18-month warranty for the whole machine and lifelong technical support.
