About us

Leading manufacturer of electric motors

Dazhong is a world-leading industrial electric motor manufacturer and professional motor supplier in China, established in 1958 with a registered capital of $31 million.

  • Servo motors
  • High- and low-voltage
  • DC motors
  • Permanent magnet
  • Explosion-proof
  • Custom special motors
  • Matched VFDs

The factory employs 2,000 people, 68% of whom are professional technicians, including 1,360 senior and master-level experts.

“Quality first and customer priority” — the principle behind the high-quality products and full-cycle services we provide to meet the ever-growing global industrial demands.

Industries

Applications

Dazhong motors run where a stoppage costs more than the equipment itself. Hover over an industry to see what the machine is actually asked to do there.

  • Metallurgy Rolling mills and roller tables, induced-draught fans and sinter plant pumps, hoisting gear in the casting bays — frequent reversals, shock loads, dust and heat. Read more
  • Coal and mining Mine hoists, belt conveyors, main ventilation fans and drainage pumps. The defining requirement is explosion protection and work in a dusty, humid atmosphere. Read more
  • Mechanical engineering Pumps, fans and compressors built into packaged equipment: the customer counts efficiency and looks at compatibility with the variable frequency drive. Read more
  • Ports and shipbuilding Port container cranes, shipboard machinery and lifting equipment. Frequent starts under load, variable speed, salt air and open yards. Read more
  • Building materials and power Cement mills and kilns, induced-draught fans at thermal power plants, water treatment equipment. Duty is continuous, so service life and maintainability come first. Read more
