YBBP series explosion-proof high-voltage variable frequency variable speed three-phase asynchronous motor


Application Scenarios

Mainly applied in petrochemical, oil and gas exploitation, pharmaceutical, coal chemical, underground coal mines and other Zone 1 and Zone 2 explosive hazardous areas, matching equipment requiring precision speed regulation and energy-saving operation including centrifugal fans, centrifugal pumps, metering pumps, screw compressors, mixers and conveyors, especially suitable for variable frequency supporting of new projects and energy-saving transformation of in-service equipment.

Product Certification

Obtained the explosion protection certificate issued by the National Supervision and Inspection Center for Explosion Protection Electrical Products, IE3 high-efficiency certification, and passed ISO9001 Quality Management System certification, fully complying with GB explosion protection series standards, variable frequency motor series standards and IEC international standards.

Manufacturer Strength

We have a professional R&D team for explosion-proof variable frequency motors and a leading domestic full-working condition test platform for variable frequency motors. We can provide exclusive matching design for different frequency converter brands and speed regulation working conditions, and support customized development of special speed, voltage and protection grade to meet the non-standard requirements of high-end variable frequency explosion-proof projects.

Global After-sales Service

We provide exclusive technical support for variable frequency explosion-proof working conditions, delivering full-process services including pre-sales working condition simulation and selection, in-sales joint commissioning test of motor and frequency converter, and after-sales whole-life cycle operation, maintenance and upgrade. We offer on-site installation and commissioning services for domestic and overseas projects, with 24/7 all-weather technical response.
