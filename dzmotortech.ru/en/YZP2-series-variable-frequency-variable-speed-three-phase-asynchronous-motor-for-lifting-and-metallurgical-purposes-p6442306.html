YZP2 series variable frequency variable speed three-phase asynchronous motor for lifting and metallurgical purposes


Application Scenarios

It is mainly applied to high-performance lifting equipment, metallurgical automation production lines, continuous casting equipment, rolling auxiliary equipment, port container cranes and other heavy industrial scenarios with high requirements for motor performance.

Product Certification

It has passed ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification, complies with GB/T 21972 Technical Specification for Variable Frequency Speed Control Three-phase Asynchronous Motors for Lifting and Metallurgical Applications. Its product performance has reached the international advanced level.

Manufacturer Strength

We have internationally advanced variable frequency motor design and manufacturing technology, and have built a leading domestic variable frequency motor performance testing center. We have strong R&D and innovation capabilities, and can provide customized high-performance variable frequency lifting and metallurgical motor solutions according to customers' high-end needs.

Global After-sales Service

We provide an exclusive high-end customer service team, offering one-to-one technical support and after-sales service for customers, including pre-project technical consultation, system scheme design, as well as post-project installation and commissioning, personnel training and regular maintenance services.
