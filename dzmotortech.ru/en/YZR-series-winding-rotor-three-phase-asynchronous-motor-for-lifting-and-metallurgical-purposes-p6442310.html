Special motor_YZR series winding rotor three-phase asynchronous motor for lifting and metallurgical purposes


Application Scenarios

It is mainly applied to various cranes, bridge cranes, gantry cranes, tower cranes, winches, rolling equipment, continuous casting equipment, hoists in the metallurgical industry and other heavy mechanical equipment requiring frequent starting and heavy-load operation.

Product Certification

It has passed ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification, complies with GB/T 13952 Technical Specification for Three-phase Asynchronous Motors for Lifting and Metallurgical Applications. All products have undergone strict overload tests and durability tests to ensure that product performance meets the requirements of heavy-duty working conditions.

Manufacturer Strength

We have a leading domestic production line and testing equipment for lifting and metallurgical motors, and have rich industry application experience. Our products are widely used in major domestic iron and steel enterprises, ports and crane manufacturers. Our R&D team can provide customized lifting and metallurgical motor solutions according to customers' special working condition requirements.

Global After-sales Service

We provide professional technical support and after-sales service for lifting and metallurgical motors, can assist customers in installation, commissioning and maintenance of motors, and provide on-site troubleshooting and spare parts supply services to ensure the normal operation of equipment.
