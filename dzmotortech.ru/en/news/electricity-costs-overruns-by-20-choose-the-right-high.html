Electricity Costs Overruns by 20%+? Choose the Right High-Efficiency Motors and Dazhong Helps You Achieve Payback in Just 1.8 Years

  • Friday, 16 January 2026
  • 0
  • 160
  • 0


In industrial production, electric motors serve as core power equipment, and their energy efficiency decline has become a key pain point driving up corporate costs. Data show that motor energy consumption accounts for more than 60% of total industrial electricity use and 35% of energy losses can be traced back to inefficient motors or flawed system designs. As a professional Chinese supplier and manufacturer, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech has deeply focused on the high-efficiency motor sector, leveraging high-quality products and energy efficiency solutions to help global enterprises overcome energy efficiency challenges and build long-term global partnerships.


I. The Corporate Cost Crisis Behind Energy Efficiency Decline
1.Uncontrolled Energy Consumption from Low-Efficiency Equipment: Motors with energy efficiency levels at IE1 or below account for over 40% of the in-service stock, resulting in an annual excess power consumption of more than 150 billion kWh. Consequently, enterprises’ electricity expenses generally exceed budgets by over 20%.

2.Energy Losses from Mismatched Loads: The efficiency of traditional motors plummets by 8%–15% when operating under low-load conditions of 30%–50%, with no-load losses consuming substantial amounts of electricity. For example, the efficiency of rolling mill motors in steel plants drops to merely 82% during low-load operation.
3.Failure of Operation and Maintenance Models to Anticipate Degraded Motor Efficiency: Over five years, motor efficiency decreases on average by 6-8%; traditional maintenance models do not provide early warning signals in time, leading to significant financial losses from unexpected downtime and unexpected motor failure.



II.Dazhong's Energy Efficiency Upgrade Solutions for High-Efficiency Electric Motors represent breakthrough innovations.
1. High-Quality Basic Designs to Provide the Basis for Energy Efficiency
Dazhong Electric Motor Tech adheres to high-quality manufacturing standards. Their products meet both China's GB30254 Level 2 energy efficiency standard as well as international IE4/IE5 regulations. By employing copper bar rotors and advanced insulation systems, we increase power density while simultaneously expanding overload capacity by 30%. Optimized heat dissipation structure design improves efficiency by 1.2% for every 10degC decrease in temperature rise, and also cuts no-load losses by 40% when compared with traditional motors. Our entire product portfolio has undergone rigorous explosion-proof and temperature resistance tests, accommodating temperatures ranging from -40degC to 120degC while covering power ranges spanning 220kW-11300 kW in order to satisfy stringent industry specifications.
