VFD Tripping? Dazhong Fixes Fast, Production Uninterrupted

  • Monday, 20 April 2026
  • 0
  • 100
  • 0

Frequent tripping of Variable Frequency Drive on production lines is one of the most common downtime risks in manufacturing, and a key challenge restricting productivity and raising operation & maintenance costs. As an experienced Chinese supplier and professional manufacturer in industrial transmission, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech delivers high quality variable frequency drives, supported by rapid fault diagnosis and full-range services. We target VFD tripping issues with fast, on-site troubleshooting to locate causes in seconds and carry out repairs without halting production. Supported by energy efficiency-focused design and flexible customized production, we have become a trusted global partnership for customers worldwide, empowering efficient industrial production with China’s intelligent manufacturing.


I. Variable Frequency Drive Tripping: Hidden Losses & Cost Wastes to Production Lines

In industrial production, Variable Frequency Drive serves as a core component for motor speed regulation and energy-saving control, widely used in fans, pumps, machine tools, mining machinery, HVAC, automated production lines and many other fields.Frequent tripping has long troubled enterprises, bringing far more losses than temporary downtime, with multiple hidden costs involved.

1.Main Causes of VFD Tripping

Tripping can be triggered by six common factors: overload, overvoltage/undervoltage, poor heat dissipation, parameter mismatch, load fluctuation, and circuit fault.

2.Impacts of Frequent Tripping

These issues directly affect business operation and production:

  • Sudden shutdown reduces productivity, delays order delivery, and can cause huge economic losses in continuous production industries;
  • Repeated tripping accelerates aging of motors and internal components of VFDs, shortens service life, raises replacement costs, and traps users in a "fault-repair-fault" cycle;
  • Traditional troubleshooting relies heavily on manual experience, which is time-consuming and inefficient, often taking hours or even days to locate the root cause, leading to high O&M costs;
  • Frequent starts and stops increase energy consumption, conflicting with enterprises’ goals of cost reduction, energy saving and low-carbon production, and weakening the energy-saving value of Variable Frequency Drive itself.



II. Dazhong Electric Motor Tech: High-Quality Chinese Manufacturer of Variable Frequency Drive

Dazhong Variable Frequency Drive adopts energy efficiency as the core design concept, perfectly combining stability and energy saving, helping enterprises cut costs and reduce carbon emissions, improve core competitiveness, and keep pace with the global trend of low-carbon production.

1.Soft Start Design

It avoids high-current impulse during motor start-up, protects motors and drives, reduces grid load and lowers starting energy consumption.

2.On-Demand Speed Regulation

It automatically adjusts motor speed based on actual working conditions, avoiding unnecessary energy waste from constant rated-speed operation.

For fans and pumps, energy saving can reach 30%–50%, greatly reducing electricity costs for long-term use.

FAQ: Common Questions About VFD Tripping

1.Why does the VFD trip immediately after starting?

A: It is usually caused by short circuit, poor grounding or wrong parameter settings. Dazhong can identify the exact cause in 1 minute via remote diagnosis without blind inspection.

2.How to solve overload tripping during operation?

A: First check if the load exceeds the rated range, then inspect for motor jamming. We provide free parameter optimization to solve overload tripping.

3.How to deal with overvoltage/undervoltage tripping?

A: It is mainly caused by grid voltage fluctuation. We can optimize circuits or install voltage stabilizers, with on-site tailored solutions from our engineers.

4.What to do if tripping is caused by poor heat dissipation?

A: Regular dust cleaning and fan inspection are needed. We provide professional O&M plans and cooling system upgrades to prevent heat-related tripping.
5.Do you support customized production?

A: Yes. We provide customization on voltage, power, communication protocol, protection level and more for various industries and working conditions.

7.Are your products high quality and up to international standards?

8.How is the energy efficiency performance of your products?

A: Our drives feature outstanding speed-regulation energy saving, up to 30%–50% for fans and pumps, helping enterprises reduce costs and carbon emissions.

9.What global partnership modes do you offer?

A: We provide agency, ODM, project customization and other modes, with full technical support and after-sales service to help partners expand markets.
