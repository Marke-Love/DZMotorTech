Explosion-Proof Motors for Mining & Metallurgy-----High Quality & High Efficiency, Global Preferred Choice

  • Monday, 20 April 2026
  • 0
  • 52
  • 0
As a professional Chinese manufacturer and supplier, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech focuses deeply on the research, development and production of explosion-proof motors tailored for the mining and metallurgy industries. We dedicate ourselves to delivering high-quality and energy-efficient explosion-proof motor products, alongside customized manufacturing solutions. We build global cooperative partnerships and provide full-process supporting services. Our comprehensive range of explosion-proof products covers all specialized models for mining and metallurgical scenarios, perfectly adapting to diverse high-risk operating environments, making us a reliable preferred supplier for mining and metallurgical enterprises worldwide.

I. Full-Spectrum Explosion-Proof Motor Portfolio: Meeting All Mining & Metallurgy Scenario Demands

As an experienced manufacturer of explosion-proof motors, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech develops a diversified lineup of explosion-proof motors for mining and metallurgical applications. All products pass authoritative certifications, suitable for various complex working conditions in this sector. The core products prioritize operational safety, energy efficiency and long-term stable performance to precisely satisfy diverse production requirements. The portfolio includes five core series (including YBKS and YFB4), fully covering all operational links in mining and metallurgical processes and meeting power supply needs of different equipment and working environments.

1.1 Ex d IIC T4 Flameproof Explosion-Proof Motor (Core Flagship Model)

Developed and certified exclusively for explosive environments in mining and metallurgy, this model complies with ATEX and IECEx international standards as well as domestic specifications for explosion-proof electrical equipment. It reaches the highest protection level for Class IIC explosive gas atmospheres, with a T4 temperature classification that limits the maximum surface temperature of the equipment to no more than 135°C, eliminating ignition risks at the source.

The motor features a high-strength forged flameproof enclosure, which can withstand internal explosion pressure without deformation or damage. Precision clearances on enclosure joint surfaces rapidly cool down flames and block external flame leakage effectively. Internally, high-temperature resistant Class F insulating materials are adopted, combined with an integrated dustproof and waterproof sealing structure. It ensures safe and stable operation in explosive atmospheres with heavy dust, high humidity and strong vibration on mining and metallurgical sites. Optimized electromagnetic design enables the motor to achieve IE3 or higher energy efficiency standards, delivering long-term energy-saving operation for general machinery and equipment in mines and metallurgical plants.



1.2 YBBP Series Low-Voltage Three-Phase Flameproof Variable-Frequency Motors (Dedicated Variable-Speed Model)

Custom-engineered to meet variable frequency speed regulation demands in mining and metallurgy, this motor pairs perfectly with our in-house high-performance current vector inverters of EV510H and EV510A series. It realizes precise speed adjustment for equipment such as mine conveyors, metallurgical melting furnaces and ore crushing machinery, reducing wear and tear caused by equipment start-stop operations and further improving overall energy efficiency.

Rated Ex d IIC T4 for explosion protection and certified to ATEX/IECEx requirements, the product boasts outstanding dustproof, moisture-proof and anti-vibration properties. It maintains stable power output regulation in flammable and explosive areas, serving as an essential power supporting component for automated production lines in mining and metallurgical industries.



1.3 YBKS Series Mining Flameproof Three-Phase Asynchronous Motors (Special Coal Mine Model)

Designed for extreme high-risk underground coal mine environments, this motor holds Ex d IIC T4 explosion-proof rating with ATEX, IECEx and Coal Safety Certification, conforming to GB 3836 series national standards. It is applicable to underground coal mining equipment including scraper conveyors, winches and transfer conveyors.

Reinforced flameproof housing and an efficient heat dissipation structure resist hazards such as underground gas accumulation, frequent mechanical vibration and severe dust erosion. Class F high-temperature resistant windings guarantee long service life and reliable performance. Meeting IE3 and above efficiency grades, the motor features excellent overload capacity and supports 24-hour continuous stable operation to ensure efficient coal mining progress.



1.4 YFB4 Series Dust Flameproof Three-Phase Asynchronous Motors (Dust Explosion-Proof Model)

Specially configured for heavy dust scenarios in mining and metallurgy, this motor carries Ex tD B21 dust explosion-proof certification in line with ATEX and IECEx norms and professional dust explosion-proof standards. It is ideal for high-concentration dust working areas like ore crushing workshops and metallurgical screening stations.

Optimized sealing and heat dissipation structures together with an anti-dust accumulation housing design prevent particulate matter from entering the motor interior and eliminate potential safety hazards triggered by dust buildup. It achieves ultra-high IE4 energy efficiency level with low operating noise and slight vibration, perfectly matching various mechanical equipment running in dense dust environments of mining and metallurgical facilities.



1.5 YBX3 Series High-Efficiency Flameproof Three-Phase Asynchronous Motors (Universal High-Efficiency Model)

A versatile high-efficiency flameproof motor for general mining and metallurgical use, rated Ex d IIC T4 and certified per ATEX/IECEx standards as well as GB 3836.1-2010 and GB 3836.2-2010 specifications. Its center height ranges from 80mm to 355mm, covering power requirements of conventional mining and metallurgical machinery.

Adopting high-efficiency iron core and winding configurations, it reaches IE3 or superior energy efficiency levels. Characterized by compact structure, easy installation, low noise and low vibration, the motor offers reliable dustproof and waterproof performance. Suited for auxiliary mining equipment and workshop power systems in metallurgical factories, it delivers remarkable cost performance across wide application scenarios.

Backed by strong independent R&D and comprehensive manufacturing capabilities, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech provides all-round customized production services for global clients in mining and metallurgy. As a trusted professional Chinese supplier, we customize core parameters including motor dimensions, rotational speed, protection grades, wiring modes and explosion-proof ratings according to clients’ equipment types, installation space, power demands and actual operating conditions (such as gas density, dust levels and ambient temperature ranges).
Whether for miniature auxiliary mining machinery or main power units of large-scale metallurgical production lines, we formulate exclusive flameproof power solutions targeted to individual needs. Energy-saving structural optimization can also be implemented upon request to further elevate overall energy utilization efficiency.

We establish a complete full-lifecycle service system to offer one-stop technical and operational support for mining and metallurgical clients, securing stable equipment operation and lowering overall operational costs. In the pre-sales phase, professional engineers deliver personalized motor selection recommendations and integrated explosion-proof system solutions based on on-site working conditions and equipment parameters. During production, we update manufacturing progress in real time and support sample testing & parameter confirmation to guarantee finished products fully comply with client requirements before delivery. After sales, we provide free on-site installation guidance and commissioning training, alongside 7×24-hour online technical support. All equipment malfunctions receive response within 48 hours,with lifelong technical consultation and maintenance services available at all times. Our comprehensive service framework underpins solid global partnerships and widespread recognition from mining and metallurgical customers around the world.

IV. Global Cooperative Partnerships: Building an International Cooperation Ecosystem

As a qualified and reliable Chinese manufacturer and supplier, Dazhong Electric Motor Tech builds long-term global partnerships relying on premium-quality explosion-proof motors, standardized service systems and an extensive product lineup (Ex d IIC T4 flameproof type, YBBP, YBKS, YFB4 and YBX3 series). Our products are exported to multiple key mining and metallurgical countries worldwide, establishing us as a top-choice explosion-proof motor supplier in the global market.

We uphold the principle of win-win cooperation with all partners, optimizing product design and certification compliance to meet localized explosion-proof regulations and industrial criteria of different regions. Our high-quality, energy-saving explosion-proof motors empower safe and efficient production operations in global mining and metallurgical industries, while we continuously expand the boundary of international business collaboration.
