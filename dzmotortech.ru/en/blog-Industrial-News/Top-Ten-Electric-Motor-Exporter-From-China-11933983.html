They are a trusted choice for global buyers who want high-quality, energy-saving and environmentally friendly motors.

In 2024, DC motor production in China expanded significantly. The figure was higher than the peak recorded in the previous year by a significant margin. The increase was largely driven by the rapid growth in the demand for high-efficiency, cost-effective and durable motors.

In value terms, DC motor exports also registered a significant rise. They stood at $8.5B in 2024, which is up by a significant margin compared to the figure registered in the previous year. The growth pace was the fastest in 2021, but waned somewhat in the subsequent years.

China’s top export markets for DC motors in 2024 were India, Vietnam and the Netherlands, which together accounted for 26% of the total exports. The other major destinations included Hong Kong SAR, South Korea and Mexico.
