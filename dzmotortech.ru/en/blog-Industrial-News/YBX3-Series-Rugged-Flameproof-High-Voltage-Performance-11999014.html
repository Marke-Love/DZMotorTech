YBX3 Series: Rugged Flameproof, High-Voltage Performance

  • Thursday, 23 April 2026
  • 0
  • 66
  • 0
With decades of in-depth experience in the explosion-proof motor field, Jiangsu Dazhong Electric Motor has independently developed and launched the YBX3 series flameproof high-voltage three-phase asynchronous motor—a core product tailored by our R&D team specifically for high-risk working conditions. Covering a power range from 315kW to 2800kW, it features core indicators including ±0.5% precise speed control, 1.2mm/s ultra-low vibration, and the maximum ExdIICT4 explosion-proof rating. Backed by Jiangsu Dazhong’s rigorous manufacturing processes, two exclusive patented technologies, and in-depth customization capabilities, it has become the core preferred choice for high-voltage explosion-proof power in high-risk working conditions. Among them, two exclusive core technologies of Dazhong safeguard the product performance: first, the patented low stray loss rotor aluminum casting densification process; second, the exclusive integrated hydraulic pressure tight welding process for high-voltage flameproof motor frames, laying a solid foundation for the core advantages of energy-saving and efficiency-optimized, operationally steady, and intrinsically safe performance from the source.



I. Energy-Saving and Efficiency-Optimized: Empowering Enterprises to Cut Costs and Boost Efficiency

This series of motors stably meets the IE3 energy efficiency level, and customized models can approach the IE4 ultra-high efficiency standard. The measured overall energy efficiency is 0.2%–0.5% higher than the Class 1 national standard of GB 30254-2024 for high-voltage motors. Supported by Dazhong’s exclusive precise electromagnetic scheme optimization technology, matched with 35WW300 high-permeability silicon steel materials and low-loss cast aluminum rotor structure, and further equipped with Dazhong’s patented low stray loss rotor aluminum casting densification process, the rotor aluminum liquid filling rate reaches over 99.7%, reducing rotor stray loss by 18% and stator loss by 12%, which further improves the overall energy efficiency performance and achieves the optimal balance between energy consumption and efficiency.

Calculated under the working condition of 1000kW/6kV with 8000 operating hours per year: the equipment can save about 530,000 kWh of electricity annually and reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 416 tons. The premium of initial equipment investment can be quickly recovered through long-term power-saving benefits within 2–3 years, fully meeting the core demands of high-risk production enterprises for energy consumption control, carbon neutrality target implementation, and long-term operating cost optimization—which is a commitment that Jiangsu Dazhong Electric Motor has adhered to for more than ten years in the explosion-proof motor industry.



II. High-Efficiency Heat Dissipation & High-Temperature Adaptation: Suitable for Complex and Severe High-Temperature Environments

Adopting the fully enclosed self-cooling structure IC411 optimized by CFD flow field simulation system, combined with the Class F (155℃) high-temperature resistant insulation protection system, the overall heat dissipation efficiency is 25% higher than that of traditional conventional models. The winding temperature rise is 15K lower than the national standard, and the steady-state winding temperature under full load is ≤120℃. It can operate continuously for a long time at an ambient temperature of 40℃ and an altitude of 1000 meters, adapting to the long-term operation needs of high-temperature and complex working conditions.

The integrated cast iron frame, evenly arranged cooling fins, dead-angle-free internal air duct, and IP55 high-level protection design can not only quickly dissipate the heat generated by equipment operation but also effectively block the erosion of dust, hydrogen sulfide, ammonia, and other corrosive media. It ensures the long-term stable operation of the motor in complex corrosive and high-temperature environments, avoids equipment shutdown caused by abnormal heat dissipation, and reduces unplanned maintenance costs.

III. Vibration-Suppressed and Heavy-Duty Adaptation: Meeting the Needs of High-Intensity Variable Frequency Operation

The whole machine adopts a reinforced rotor structure and alloy quenched and tempered steel shaft. Through finite element global simulation analysis, the overall rigidity of the frame and end cover is optimized. Combined with the patented low stray loss rotor aluminum casting densification process, the overall vibration value of the equipment is controlled within ≤1.2mm/s, which is better than the national general standard of 2.3mm/s, and the operating noise is controlled within 85dB(A). It fundamentally avoids equipment abnormalities such as premature bearing aging, loose fixing bolts, and pipeline resonance cracking caused by long-term high-frequency operation, ensuring the smooth operation under heavy-duty working conditions.

With a torque density of 2.8N·m/kg and a starting torque multiple of 2.2 times, it can directly drive heavy loads such as fans, compressors, and crushing equipment to start stably without additional soft-start devices. The self-developed dual-loop variable frequency control system achieves ±0.5% precise speed regulation, 10%–100% rated speed wide-range regulation, and a signal response time of ≤50ms. It perfectly adapts to the production needs such as DCS closed-loop control, precise pressure and flow regulation, and multi-equipment synchronous linkage under variable frequency conditions, ensuring a stable, accurate, and efficient speed regulation process that adapts to high-intensity variable frequency operation scenarios.

IV. Long-Service-Life and Full-Range Protection: Ensuring Continuous and Stable Production

Dazhong Electric Motor implements a full-process quality control system, with the equipment’s fault-free operation cycle ≥20,000 hours and the overall design service life ≥15 years. The long-acting wear-resistant lubricating bearing is matched with an optimized large-space grease injection structure, which extends the equipment maintenance grease injection cycle by 1.5 times, greatly reducing the daily maintenance workload and later operation and maintenance investment, and realizing low-cost operation and maintenance throughout the equipment’s life cycle.

The flameproof structure strictly complies with three major domestic and foreign authoritative standards: GB/T 3836.2-2021, IEC60079-1, and EN50018. Relying on Dazhong’s decades of experience in explosion-proof technology, it adopts the exclusive integrated hydraulic pressure tight welding process for high-voltage flameproof motor frames. The spigot of the frame and end cover is clamped and automatically precision-welded at one time, with the coaxiality controlled within ≤0.02mm (better than the national standard of 0.05mm). The flameproof joint of the whole machine has zero leakage and zero deformation, and passes the water pressure and air tightness tests for the highest ExdIICT4 explosion-proof rating at one time. The explosion-proof reliability is 40% higher than that of traditional processes, and the repair rate is close to zero. The explosion-proof protection level fully covers various scenarios such as ExdI Mb for coal mines, ExdIIBT4 Gb for conventional chemical industry, and ExdIICT4 Gb for high-risk chemical industry, blocking the intrusion of flammable and explosive gases from the structural level and comprehensively building a production safety barrier for high-risk working conditions.

The stator winding adopts Dazhong’s customized VPI vacuum pressure impregnation exclusive process, which greatly enhances the insulation and voltage shock resistance. It can stably resist ±10% grid voltage fluctuation and 3rd to 15th harmonic interference from variable frequency, highly adapting to the 24-hour continuous production mode and maintaining the continuous operation of the production line—which is a commitment that Jiangsu Dazhong Electric Motor has adhered to for more than ten years in the explosion-proof motor industry.

V. Compact Structure & Full-Dimensional Customization: Suitable for Multi-Scenario Installation and Renovation

The frame specifications cover the full range of H355–H560, with a power range of 315kW–2800kW, supporting multiple rated voltages of 3000V, 6000V, and 10000V, and optional pole numbers from 2 to 12. It can accurately match the power supporting needs of core production equipment in most high-risk industries without additional adjustment of equipment adaptability.

Adopting the IMB35 flange mounting form, compared with traditional high-voltage motors of the same power specification, it has a 15% smaller volume and 10% lighter weight. It can directly replace old equipment in situ without modifying the installation base and civil engineering structure, effectively reducing the capital investment and implementation cycle of equipment upgrading and renovation, and improving the efficiency of equipment renewal.

At the same time, comprehensive customized R&D supporting services are provided. It can customize outdoor protection type, tropical humid type, special anti-corrosion type according to needs, and support personalized configurations such as non-standard voltage, exclusive speed, high-altitude adaptation, and -20℃ low-temperature start-up, accurately fitting the differentiated use needs of different site environments and operating conditions, and realizing scenario-specific exclusive adaptation.



VI. Multi-Industry Adaptation & Professional Empowerment: Facilitating Equipment Upgrading in High-Risk Industries

Integrating the multiple advantages of energy-saving and efficiency-optimized, full-range protection, operationally steady, and easy installation, the YBX3 series high-voltage flameproof motor is widely applicable to core power equipment in multiple industries, providing exclusive power support for various high-risk production fields and specifically solving the power challenges of different industries:
Petrochemical Industry: Suitable for flameproof centrifugal fans, process compressors, hydrogenation pumps, and rectification tower driving devices. It can withstand corrosive media and flammable and explosive environments, maintain the long-term stable operation of equipment, reduce unplanned shutdowns, and ensure the continuous progress of chemical production;
Coal Mining Industry: Suitable for main ventilation fans, belt conveyors, underground crushers, and drainage pump sets. It adapts to the mine gas environment and humid operating conditions, has strong heavy-duty starting performance, ensures the safe and continuous underground operation, and avoids the risk of underground power failures;
Metallurgy and Electric Power Industry: Suitable for induced draft fans, rolling mills, dust removal fans, and coal mills. It can withstand high-temperature and dusty operating environments, has excellent vibration suppression performance and strong anti-harmonic interference capability, meets the requirements of long-term high-intensity operation, and supports the efficient production of the metallurgy and electric power industry;
In addition, the equipment is also applicable to flameproof power equipment in fields such as natural gas transmission, coal chemical industry, and building materials manufacturing. It provides safe, controllable, energy-saving, efficient, and highly adaptable complete power solutions for all types of high-risk industries, boosting the stable and high-quality operation of enterprises.
For long-term development, Jiangsu Dazhong Electric Motor will continue to focus on the field of flameproof high-voltage motors, with higher energy efficiency level, lower vibration performance, stronger flameproof protection, longer service life, and intelligent monitoring upgrading as the core R&D directions. Relying on mature manufacturing technology, two exclusive patented processes, and customization service capabilities, it will continue to deliver high-performance power equipment to various high-risk industries, work together with various production enterprises to consolidate the foundation of production safety, and achieve the coordinated development of energy conservation, consumption reduction, efficiency improvement, and high-quality development—which is a commitment that Jiangsu Dazhong Electric Motor has adhered to for more than ten years in the explosion-proof motor industry.
