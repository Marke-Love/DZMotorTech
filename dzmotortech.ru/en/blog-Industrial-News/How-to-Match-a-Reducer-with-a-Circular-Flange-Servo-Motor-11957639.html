How to Match a Reducer with a Circular Flange Servo Motor?

  • Thursday, 13 November 2025
  • 0
  • 133
  • 0
In industrial automated production lines, circular flange servo motors play an important role due to their characteristics of convenient installation and stable power. Such motors often need to work in conjunction with reducers to achieve precise control through speed reduction and torque increase. However, many enterprises still face problems such as parameter mismatch and performance waste when matching them. A scientific matching scheme has become the key to improving equipment efficiency.

The primary core of matching a circular flange servo motor with a reducer is parameter matching. It is necessary to first clarify the rated torque of the motor and the load requirement. For example, when a 250W circular flange servo motor has an output torque of 2NM, matching it with a planetary reducer with a speed ratio of 16, combined with a transmission efficiency of 80%, can achieve an output torque of 25.6NM. It is necessary to ensure that this value is lower than the rated load of the reducer to ensure safety. At the same time, the selection of the reduction ratio must take into account both the motor speed and the load speed to avoid the motor entering a non-high-efficiency working area.

Flange size adaptation is the basic hardware requirement for matching circular flange servo motors. Different models of circular flange servo motors correspond to fixed flange specifications. For instance, a flange size of 60mm is suitable for 200W/400W servo motors, while a 90mm flange matches 750W servo motors. The input end of the reducer must fit these sizes accurately. If the flange size does not match, it is easy to cause installation deviation, affect transmission accuracy, and even cause equipment vibration.

Precision requirements determine the direction of reducer type selection. Circular flange servo motors have the advantage of high-precision control, so the backlash index of the reducer must be considered when matching: in the machine tool feed system, the circular flange servo motor should be matched with a planetary reducer with a backlash of less than 3arcmin; for robot joint drive, a high-precision reducer with a backlash of ≤1arcmin is required to ensure the positioning error is within ±0.02mm.

In practical applications, installation and compatibility issues must also be considered. The connection methods between circular flange servo motors and reducers are divided into direct connection type and split type. For direct connection, the coaxiality error must be controlled within 0.05mm, while the split type can absorb slight deviations through a coupling. In addition, models such as the PX series planetary reducer have become common matches for circular flange servo motors of 250W and below due to their strong adaptability.

Industry technicians emphasize that the matching of circular flange servo motors and reducers is not a simple combination, but requires comprehensive multi-dimensional considerations such as load, precision, and installation conditions. A reasonable matching scheme can fully release the performance of the circular flange servo motor, not only avoiding power waste, but also prolonging the service life of the entire transmission system, providing stable support for automated production.
