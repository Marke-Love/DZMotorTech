Selection

What we check before quoting

A specification or the nameplate of the motor to be replaced is enough.

  • 01

    Machine and duty

    Driven machine

    Windlass, winch, crane, pump, fan, compressor, purifier or thruster.

    Duty

    Starting under load, frequent starts, continuous running, speed control.

  • 02

    Ship supply

    Voltage and frequency

    380 V / 50 Hz, 440 V / 60 Hz, 690 V or another shipboard voltage.

    Speed control

    Direct-on-line starting or a frequency converter, control range.

  • 03

    Conditions

    Location

    Open deck, engine room or hazardous space.

    Protection

    IP55, IP56 or IP65 ingress protection, salt mist resistance, insulation class.

  • 04

    Certification and mounting

    Class society

    CCS, ABS, DNV, BV or LR — whichever the vessel project requires.

    Mounting

    Frame size, mounting arrangement, brake, temperature sensors.

No specification? Send a photo of the installed motor nameplate — we will select a replacement from it.

How it works

The selection process

  1. We receive the data

    A specification, a questionnaire or a photo of the motor nameplate and the name of the driven machine.

  2. Technical check

    Our specialist checks the power, supply voltage and frequency, ingress protection, mounting dimensions and certification requirements.

  3. We propose a model

    The motor series and version: ingress protection, insulation, brake and sensors, class society certificate.

    We send a commercial proposal with price and lead time.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked

What should I send to get a quotation?

A specification or a questionnaire. If you have neither, send the nameplate of the installed motor and the name of the driven machine: power, ship supply voltage and frequency, and the class society required.

Which class society approvals do the motors carry?

The factory lists approvals from CCS, ABS, DNV, BV and LR. The set depends on the series — we will confirm it against your specification.

What voltages and frequencies are the marine motors built for?

The range is 220–690 V at 50 and 60 Hz, including 380 V / 50 Hz, 440 V / 60 Hz and 690 V. The permitted supply voltage deviation is ±10%.

What ingress protection do the marine motors have?

IP55 as standard, with IP56 and IP65 to order. Windings and frames are built for salt mist and humidity.

Are there deck machinery motors with speed control?

Yes. Windlass and mooring winch motors run with a frequency converter over 5–100 Hz; an electromagnetic brake is optional. We can also select the drive — see variable frequency drives.

Are there motors for pumping stations and hydropower plants?

Yes. For water conservancy pumping stations — 140 kW – 28.5 MW at 380 V – 13.8 kV. For hydropower plants and pump stations — high-voltage 160–8,000 kW and large synchronous 1–65 MW. Large water pump motors go up to 50 MW.

What is the lead time?

It depends on the series and the version and is stated in the quotation together with the price. Motors are supplied to order.
