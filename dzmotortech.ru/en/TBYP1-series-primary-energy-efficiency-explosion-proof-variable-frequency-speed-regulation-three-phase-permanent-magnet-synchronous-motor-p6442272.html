TBYP1 series primary energy efficiency explosion-proof variable frequency speed regulation three-phase permanent magnet synchronous motor


Application Scenarios

Mainly used in industries with flammable and explosive hazardous media such as petroleum, chemical, coal mining, natural gas, pharmaceutical, coatings, etc., suitable for driving explosion-proof pumps, fans, compressors, agitators, conveyors and other equipment.

Product Certification and Guarantee

This series of motors has passed Ex d ⅡC T4 Gb/Ex tD A21 IP65 T135℃ explosion-proof certification from the National Quality Supervision and Inspection Center for Explosion-proof Electrical Products, ISO9001 Quality Management System certification, and ISO14001 Environmental Management System certification. All explosion-proof motors have a unique product file established, and we provide lifelong technical support and spare parts supply services.
