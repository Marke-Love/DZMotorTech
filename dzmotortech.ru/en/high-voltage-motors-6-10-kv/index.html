Versions

Synchronous and induction motors

  • Synchronous

    For high-power drives running continuously.

    • T series efficiency — above 97 % at rated load
    • Adjustable power factor: the motor compensates reactive power in the network
    • Low speed with high torque — without a gearbox

  • Induction

    Squirrel-cage rotor — for pumps and fans, wound rotor — for heavy starting.

    • Wound rotor: high starting torque at low starting current
    • Frequent starts at full load without shocks to the transmission
    • Modular series for pumping stations with reinforced bearings for axial load

  • ID fans

    Draught machines of boiler houses and process furnaces.

  • Mills

    Equipment with heavy starting and cyclic loads.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked

What voltages and power ratings do you supply?

The main versions are 6 and 10 kV. Standard high-voltage series cover roughly 160 to 10,000 kW, and the series for pumping stations 140 to 28,500 kW and up to 13.8 kV. The exact version is selected for your machine and operating conditions.

Synchronous or induction motor — which to choose?

For pumps, fans and ID fans a squirrel-cage induction motor is usually enough. The synchronous T series pays off at high power: efficiency above 97 %, adjustable power factor and low-speed operation without a gearbox. For heavy starting under load, wound rotor motors are used.

What should I send so that you can select a motor?

A specification or a questionnaire. If you have neither — a photo of the nameplate or the datasheet of the motor to be replaced, plus details of the machine: power, voltage, speed, duty, mounting and ambient conditions.

How much does a motor cost and when will we get it?

High-voltage motors are built to order, so the price and the manufacturing time are calculated from your specification and sent with the technical proposal.

What documents do you provide?

The factory holds quality, environmental and occupational health management certificates, an after-sales service certificate and invention patents — they are published on the documents page. The document set for a particular shipment is agreed when the order is placed.

Who is the manufacturer and who is responsible for delivery?

The motors are built by the Dazhong Motor plant in China: operating since 1958, 2,000 employees, deliveries to more than 60 countries. The contract and delivery in Russia are handled by DZMotorTech LLC, office in Saint Petersburg.

Can you find a replacement for an imported motor?

Yes. Send the nameplate or the old model — we will match an equivalent by power, voltage, speed, duty and mounting dimensions. There is a separate page for this: ABB and Siemens motor replacement.
