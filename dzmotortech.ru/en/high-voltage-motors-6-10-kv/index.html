Questions and answers

Frequently asked

What voltages and power ratings do you supply?

The main versions are 6 and 10 kV. Standard high-voltage series cover 160 to 10,000 kW, and the series for pumping stations 140 to 28,500 kW and up to 13.8 kV. The exact version is selected for your machine and operating conditions.

What should I send so that you can select a motor?

A specification or a questionnaire. If you have neither — a photo of the nameplate or the datasheet of the motor to be replaced, plus details of the machine: power, voltage, speed, duty, mounting and ambient conditions.

How much does a motor cost and when will we get it?

High-voltage motors are built to order, so the price and the manufacturing time are calculated from your specification and sent with the technical proposal.

What documents do you provide?

The plant is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, the products to CE, UL and ATEX. Copies are published on the certificates page. The document set for a particular shipment is agreed when the order is placed.

Who is the manufacturer and who is responsible for delivery?

The motors are built by the Dazhong Motor plant in China: operating since 1958, 2,000 employees, motors in operation in more than 60 countries. The contract and delivery in Russia are handled by DZMotorTech LLC, office in Saint Petersburg.

Can you find a replacement for an imported motor?

Yes. Send the nameplate or the old model — we will match an equivalent by power, voltage, speed, duty and mounting dimensions. There is a separate page for this: ABB and Siemens motor replacement.
