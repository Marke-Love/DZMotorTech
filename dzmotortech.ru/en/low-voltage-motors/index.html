Selection

What we check before quoting

A specification or the nameplate of the motor to be replaced is enough.

  • 01

    Motor and machine

    Ratings

    Rated power, number of poles or speed, supply voltage and frequency.

    Driven machine

    Pump, fan, compressor, conveyor, machine tool. Constant speed or operation from a frequency converter.

  • 02

    Class and speed control

    Efficiency

    IE3, IE4 or IE5 — according to project requirements and duty.

    Variable speed

    Frequency range, constant torque or constant power, whether an encoder and a brake are needed.

  • 03

    Design

    Mounting

    Frame size, mounting arrangement and fixing dimensions — IEC or NEMA.

    Protection

    IP rating, insulation class, temperature sensors.

  • 04

    Site and replacement

    Operating conditions

    Explosive gas or dust area, temperature, dust and humidity.

    Replacement

    Make and nameplate of the installed motor — we select an equivalent by power, speed and mounting dimensions.

No specification? Send a photo of the installed motor nameplate — we will select a replacement from it.

How it works

The selection process

  1. We receive the data

    A specification, a questionnaire or a photo of the motor nameplate and the name of the driven machine.

  2. Technical check

    Our specialist checks the power, speed, voltage, efficiency class and mounting dimensions.

  3. We propose a model

    The motor series and version: ingress protection, cooling method, mounting and, if required, explosion protection.

  4. Price and lead time

    We send a commercial proposal with price and lead time.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked

Can you offer an equivalent to a motor from another manufacturer?

Yes. The series are built to standard mounting dimensions: for YE5 the factory states direct replacement of standard motors with no foundation rework. Send a photo of the nameplate. ABB and Siemens replacement has a page of its own.

What should I send to get a quotation?

A specification or a questionnaire. If you have neither, send the nameplate of the installed motor and the name of the driven machine: power, speed, voltage and mounting arrangement.

Which efficiency classes are available?

IE3 — the YE3 and YE3P series, IE4 — YE4P and the explosion-proof YBX5, IE5 — YE5 and YE5P. According to the factory, YE5 efficiency is 3–5% above IE3.

Are there motors for operation with a frequency converter?

Yes: YE3P (0.55–1000 kW), YE4P (0.12–450 kW, 10–60 Hz), YE5P (0.55–315 kW, 5–100 Hz) and the explosion-proof YBBP (0.75–1120 kW, 5–100 Hz). We can also select the drive — see variable frequency drives.

Are explosion-proof versions available?

Yes. For gas atmospheres — YBX5, YBE4 and the inverter-duty YBBP, up to 1140 V. For dust zones — YFB3 (Zones 21/22) and YFB4 (Zones 20/21/22). To NEMA standards — the NEPX series.

What voltages are the motors built for?

YE3P and YE4P — up to 1000 V; YE5, YE5P, YBX5, YBE4, YBBP and YFB4 — up to 1140 V; YFB3 — up to 720 V. NEMA series: NEPM — 208–230/460 V, 460 V, 380 V at 50 and 60 Hz; NEMA D — 208–230/460 V and 575 V at 60 Hz.

What is the lead time?

It depends on the series and the version and is stated in the quotation together with the price. Motors are supplied to order.
