About the company in detail

Electric motors and engineering solutions: supply, integration, control

We are a company that combines the role of an electric motor distributor with that of an engineering developer. We specialise in building complete packages around a motor, a variable frequency drive and software. This approach lets us treat the drive as a single system and make all of its components work together for the customer’s task.

Experience and team

Our success rests on a team with ten years of hands-on experience. Over that time we have built up the knowledge needed to deliver projects with confidence: from choosing the equipment to tuning the control algorithms. The team understands what high-power electric drives require and knows how to apply that in specific technical solutions.

Electric motors: the ranges we have worked in

The projects we have delivered used asynchronous motors rated from 450 to 1,000 kW. They were fed from a three-phase AC supply of 380 to 690 V at 50/60 Hz. We know the requirements for equipment in this range well and can match drive parameters correctly. Our competence is not limited to these ratings, though: we are ready to consider other power ranges where the task calls for it.

An integrated approach: motor + VFD + software

Our core competence is integrated solutions in which three elements work as a whole:

the motor — the power core of the drive; the variable frequency drive (VFD) — the device that supplies and regulates the motor; the software — the logic and algorithms that control the VFD and therefore the motor.

We tie these components into a single package: the motor and the VFD are selected and matched on electrical and functional parameters, while the software sets the required operating modes. This format takes the specifics of the application into account from the start and produces a predictable result.

  • the motor — the power core of the drive;
  • the variable frequency drive (VFD) — the device that supplies and regulates the motor;
  • the software — the logic and algorithms that control the VFD and therefore the motor.

Software: our own development for VFD control

A separate strand of our work is developing our own software to control the variable frequency drive. We write the algorithms and logic required for the specified modes and set the parameters and sequences so the drive operates within the defined task. This approach keeps hardware and software consistent and delivers exactly the control functions the customer needs.

Delivered projects and geography

Our experience is backed by completed installations: more than 10 successful projects in different countries and regions. In Europe — Germany, France and Belgium; in Asia — Japan, two projects in South Korea, China and Indonesia; in the Middle East — Qatar; in Russia — Moscow, Chelyabinsk and Kemerovo; in Africa — Libya. This spread shows that our solutions meet a wide variety of local regulatory requirements and can operate in different climate zones.

What we offer

We offer our competence for developing an integrated solution — a variable frequency drive plus its control program — for the customer’s specific task. Within such a solution we:

select the motor and the VFD against the required characteristics; make sure these components match each other; develop the software that controls the VFD and the motor, based on the specified modes and scenarios.

Our approach is built so the customer receives a complete drive system in which every part does its job and all the elements work in concert.

If you need a variable frequency drive and control software built around your task, we are ready to propose a solution based on our ten years of experience and a record of successfully delivered projects.

  • select the motor and the VFD against the required characteristics;
  • make sure these components match each other;
  • develop the software that controls the VFD and the motor, based on the specified modes and scenarios.

Need a drive for a specific task?

We will select the motor and the variable frequency drive, match them to each other and write the control program for your operating modes.
