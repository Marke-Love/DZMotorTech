    A motor manufacturer you can rely on

    Dazhong Motor is a national high-technology enterprise and a leading supplier of drive solutions for oil and gas, metallurgy and mechanical engineering. Our motors run in more than 60 countries and regions across Europe and the Americas.

    We advance electric drive technology, build long-term partnerships through individual support and made-to-task solutions, and promote energy-efficient drives as a route to carbon neutrality.

    Need a motor built for your conditions?

    We will match the version to your duty and operating conditions — from general industrial series to explosion-proof and special-purpose machines.
