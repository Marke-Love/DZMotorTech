Industries

Applications

Five industries where Dazhong motors run in operating plants: metallurgy, coal mining, mechanical engineering, ports and shipbuilding, building materials and power. For each one — the duty the drive has to handle, and the matching series from the catalogue.

  • 10 millionmotors per year
  • 14 million kWannual capacity
  • up to 10 kVvoltage of the high-voltage series
  • 50+countries in the supply network

01 / 05

Metallurgy

Rolling mills and roller tables, induced-draught fans and pumps at sinter plants, hoisting gear in the casting bays. The machine has to take frequent reversals and shock loads, and to run in dust and next to sources of heat.

Site conditions

  • Frequent reversals acceleration and braking under load
  • Shock loads mill stands and roller tables
  • Dust and mill scale sinter plants, casting bays
  • Heat on site operation next to sources of heat

What we supply

  • YGP variable frequency roller table motors
  • High-voltage motors
  • High-temperature motors

02 / 05

Coal and mining

Mine hoists, belt conveyors, main ventilation fans and drainage pumps. The defining requirement is explosion protection and steady operation in a dusty, humid atmosphere.

Site conditions

  • Explosive atmosphere methane and coal dust
  • Heavy dust loading conveyors and main ventilation
  • Humid atmosphere drainage pumps and mine shafts

What we supply

  • YWKK high-voltage non-sparking motors
  • Explosion and dust proof motors
  • Special-purpose versions

03 / 05

Mechanical engineering

Pumps, fans and compressors built into packaged equipment. Here the motor is part of someone else’s product, so the customer counts efficiency and looks at compatibility with the variable frequency drive.

Site conditions

  • Efficiency comes first the motor is costed inside the product
  • Driven by an inverter VFD compatibility and steep voltage fronts
  • Frame size and mounting building into packaged equipment

What we supply

  • YE series energy-efficient motors
  • Variable frequency drives
  • Low-voltage motors

04 / 05

Ports and shipbuilding

Port container cranes, shipboard machinery and lifting equipment. The duty is frequent starts under load and variable-speed operation; the environment is salt air and open yards.

Site conditions

  • Starts under load container cranes and winches
  • Variable speed precise positioning of the load
  • Salt air open quaysides and decks

What we supply

  • YZ series lifting motors
  • Marine motors
  • DC motors

05 / 05

Building materials and power

Cement mills and kilns, induced-draught fans at thermal power plants, water treatment equipment. Operation is continuous and an unplanned stop is expensive, so service life and maintainability come first.

Site conditions

  • Continuous duty kilns, mills, induced-draught fans
  • Cost of downtime an unplanned stop is expensive
  • Abrasive dust cement, ash, slurry

What we supply

  • High-voltage motors
  • Permanent magnet motors
  • TPTYC variable frequency PM synchronous motors

Do not see your industry?

Describe the mechanism, the duty cycle and the site conditions — we will match a version and propose an equivalent if the frame size you need is not in the standard range.
