About us

A leading maker of high-precision industrial electric motors

Dazhong designs and builds machines with efficiency engineered in: high-efficiency IE3–IE5 series, explosion-proof versions, permanent magnet synchronous motors, servo drives and high-voltage machines. Below is the company’s story in six chapters — what stands behind those words.

  • 1958the plant was founded
  • 45%of the first-class energy efficiency market
  • 61patents, 18 of them for inventions
  • 60+countries in the sales network

Company history

How Dazhong got to where it is today

Six chapters — from the product range to the global service network. The scale shows where you are as you read, and lets you jump to any chapter.

01

Complete solutions

Dazhong offers complete electric motor solutions — from standard energy-efficient machines to versions designed for a specific environment and a specific task.

  • High-efficiency electric motorsmeeting the IE3/IE4/IE5 standards
  • Motors for new energy sourceselectric vehicles and renewable energy systems
  • Explosion-proof motorsfully certified for hazardous environments
  • Specialised solutionsservo motors and high-voltage motors
02

Market position

One of the top three Chinese motor manufacturers by output and financial performance, the company holds 45% of the market for first-class energy efficiency motors and leads the segment of explosion-proof solutions for petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals and mining.

TOP 3among Chinese manufacturers by output
45%of the first-class energy efficiency market
No. 1in the explosion-proof segment
03

Research and development

Pursuing a strategy of innovation-led growth, the company has increased its research and development investment by 45% over the past five years.

Dazhong takes an active part in drafting national and industry standards — that is, it shapes the technical rules the whole industry works by.

04

International compliance

International compliance is confirmed by ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, CE, UL and ATEX certificates and by China’s national energy efficiency mark. This secures unhindered access to the markets of Europe, North America, South-East Asia and the Middle East.

  • ISO 9001quality management
  • ISO 14001environmental management
  • ISO 45001occupational health and safety
  • CEthe European Union market
  • ULthe North American market
  • ATEXexplosion-proof versions
  • CELChina’s energy efficiency mark
05

Environmental responsibility

Every product meets or exceeds the IE3 standard. IoT-based smart manufacturing systems monitor energy use precisely and cut waste.

IE3+the minimum class across the whole range
90%of production materials are recyclable
IoTmonitoring of energy use in production
06

Global network

The global sales network covers more than 60 countries. Our own service centres in Europe and North America provide uninterrupted round-the-clock support.

Dazhong Electric Motor Tech Ltd. continues to redefine industry standards, combining heritage, technical mastery and a customer-focused approach, and driving progress in industrial automation and clean energy around the world.
