About us

A leading maker of high-precision industrial electric motors

Dazhong designs and builds machines with efficiency engineered in: high-efficiency IE3–IE5 series, explosion-proof versions, permanent magnet synchronous motors, servo drives and high-voltage machines. Below is what stands behind those words: a motor cutaway part by part, and the company’s story in six chapters.

  • 1958the plant was founded
  • 45%of the first-class energy efficiency market
  • 61patents, 18 of them for inventions
  • 50+countries in the sales network

Cutaway

What is inside a motor

Every part is a separate engineering problem and a separate line in the catalogue. Pick a part from the list, or click it directly on the cutaway.

  • The enclosure defines the conditions the machine can work in: fully certified explosion-proof versions go to petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals and mining. In this segment the company is one of the market leaders.

    • Explosion protection
    • ATEX · CE · UL
    • Aggressive environments
    Special versions

  • A laminated stack of silicon electrical steel — this is where iron losses are born. In-house stamping is what holds the IE3–IE5 efficiency classes; first-class motors account for 45% of the company’s market.

    • IE3 · IE4 · IE5
    • Silicon steel
    • In-house stamping
    Energy-efficient series

  • The copper in the slots and the insulation system determine heating and service life. Winding is done in a dedicated workshop, and the insulation is rated for supply from a variable frequency drive with its steep voltage fronts.

    • Copper winding
    • VFD operation
    • Insulation class F/H
    Low-voltage motors

  • A gap of fractions of a millimetre is what precision machining exists for. This is also the boundary between an induction machine and a permanent magnet synchronous one — the latter underpins solutions for electric transport and renewable energy.

    • Permanent magnets
    • New energy
    • Precise air gap
    Permanent magnet motors

  • The squirrel cage and its fit on the shaft go through dynamic balancing — without it vibration eats away bearings and service life. For high-voltage machines the balancing requirements are the strictest.

    • Dynamic balancing
    • High-voltage machines
    • Vibration control
    High-voltage motors

  • Fit accuracy and shaft stiffness come to the fore where controllability matters more than power: in servo drives, where the motor holds position to within arc minutes.

    • Servo drives
    • Positioning accuracy
    • Bearing service life
    Servo drives

Company history

How Dazhong got to where it is today

Six chapters — from the product range to the global service network. The scale shows where you are as you read, and lets you jump to any chapter.

01

Complete solutions

Dazhong offers complete electric motor solutions — from standard energy-efficient machines to versions designed for a specific environment and a specific task.

  • High-efficiency electric motorsmeeting the IE3/IE4/IE5 standards
  • Motors for new energy sourceselectric vehicles and renewable energy systems
  • Explosion-proof motorsfully certified for hazardous environments
  • Specialised solutionsservo motors and high-voltage motors
02

Market position

One of the top three Chinese motor manufacturers by output and financial performance, the company holds 45% of the market for first-class energy efficiency motors and leads the segment of explosion-proof solutions for petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals and mining.

TOP 3among Chinese manufacturers by output
45%of the first-class energy efficiency market
No. 1in the explosion-proof segment

Research and development

Pursuing a strategy of innovation-led growth, the company has increased its research and development investment by 45% over the past five years.

Dazhong takes an active part in drafting national and industry standards — that is, it shapes the technical rules the whole industry works by.

04

International compliance

International compliance is confirmed by ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, CE, UL and ATEX certificates and by China’s national energy efficiency mark. This secures unhindered access to the markets of Europe, North America, South-East Asia and the Middle East.

  • ISO 9001quality management
  • ISO 14001environmental management
  • ISO 45001occupational health and safety

05

Environmental responsibility

Every product meets or exceeds the IE3 standard. IoT-based smart manufacturing systems monitor energy use precisely and cut waste.

Global network

The global sales network covers more than 50 countries. Our own service centres in Europe and North America provide uninterrupted round-the-clock support.
