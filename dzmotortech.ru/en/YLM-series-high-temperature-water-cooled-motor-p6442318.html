YLM series high-temperature water-cooled motor


Application Scenarios

It is mainly applied to extreme high-temperature industrial scenarios with ambient temperatures exceeding 60℃, including heating furnaces, annealing furnaces and sintering machines in the metallurgical industry, melting furnaces and annealing kilns in the glass industry, kiln equipment in the ceramics industry, as well as high-temperature reactors and drying equipment in the chemical industry.

Product Certification

It has passed ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification, complies with GB 18613-2020 National Standard for Energy Efficiency Limits and Grades of Electric Motors. All products have undergone strict high-temperature environment simulation tests and full-performance factory inspections to ensure the reliability and stability of each motor.

Manufacturer Strength

With nearly 30 years of R&D and manufacturing experience in special-purpose motors, we have built a leading domestic high-temperature environment simulation laboratory and a full-performance motor testing center. We have full capabilities for independent water cooling structure design, high-temperature electromagnetic scheme optimization, full-process manufacturing and full-performance testing, and can provide one-to-one customized motor solutions for high-temperature working conditions.

Global After-sales Service

We provide 7×24 all-weather technical support and rapid response services, equipped with a professional high-temperature motor installation, commissioning and maintenance team. We can provide on-site installation guidance, regular inspection and troubleshooting services, minimizing customers' equipment downtime risks and production losses.
