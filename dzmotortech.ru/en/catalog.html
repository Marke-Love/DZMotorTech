A factory, not a reseller

Dazhong is among the top three Chinese motor manufacturers by output and holds 45% of the domestic market for first-class energy-efficient machines.

Certified for the target market

ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, CE, UL and ATEX — cleared for Europe, North America, South-East Asia and the Middle East.

Explosion protection as its own line

Fully certified Ex versions for petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals and mining, not an add-on to a general-purpose series.

IE3 efficiency and above
